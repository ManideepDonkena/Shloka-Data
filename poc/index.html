Playback padding is clamped to the real gap, so auditioning a word never bleeds into the next one — 48% of word gaps are under the default 150 ms tail. A gap under 50 ms in the table means the two words run together with no detectable pause, so that cut is guesswork worth checking.
“Hear start / end” plays a short window across that boundary and zooms the waveform to it, so you can tell whether the cut lands on the voice. You can also drag the START or END line directly on the waveform.
