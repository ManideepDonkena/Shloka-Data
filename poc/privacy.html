Privacy Policy

Shloka Learn (website and Android app) · last updated 5 November 2026

Shloka Learn helps you learn Bhagavad-gītā verses by heart. This page says what the app stores, why, and how to delete it. In short: no ads, no tracking, no selling of data, and recordings of your voice are not kept.

What is stored, and why

DataWhenWhyWhere
Name (optional), username, password (stored only as a salted hash)If you create an accountSo you can sign in on any deviceOur server
Verses you marked or learned, practice scores, the time of each attemptWhile you are signed inTo show your progress, stars and streakOur server
Settings, marks, stars, streak, and a sign-in tokenAlwaysSo the app remembers you on this deviceOnly on your device
Verses and audio you have opened (Android app)When you open themSo they play without internet laterOnly on your device

Without an account (or as a guest) nothing that identifies you is stored on the server.

Microphone

  • The microphone is used only when you switch on Mic feedback, and only during "Your turn".
  • Your recording is first checked on your device. To check the words, that one recording (a few seconds) is sent to our scoring server, compared with the words of the verse, and deleted right away; only the score is kept.
  • No recording is ever stored, shared, or used to train any model.

Other services the app talks to

  • Render (hosting, Singapore) and Cloudflare D1 (database) store the account data above for us.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces.
  • YouTube: the "Class on this verse" videos are played from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube's own privacy policy applies when you play one.
  • Spoken meanings use your device's own text-to-speech.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. An account needs only a username, so we do not ask for an e-mail address, phone number or age. Teachers can see the names, usernames and practice scores of their students.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Student → Delete my account removes your account, marks and scores from the server at once.
  • Data kept only on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • If you cannot sign in any more, write to the contact below with your username and we will delete it.
