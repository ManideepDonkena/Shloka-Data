How to learn a verse

  1. Pick a verseTap “BG 1.1 ▾” at the top to choose a chapter and verse. Use ‹ › or the big Next verse button to move on.
  2. Choose the size of the partVerse, Halves, Pādas or Words. Tap any word to hear that part. Meanings are shown under each word.
  3. Press ▶ to practiseYou hear a part, then it is Your turn to chant it back (the blue bar is your time). It repeats, then moves to the next part.
  4. Tune it in ⚙ SettingsGap length, repeats, speed (pitch stays natural), “Hold the end” for long vowels, Build-up (replay everything so far and chant it all), text size.
  5. Get feedback with 🎤Switch the mic on, use headphones, and chant in your turn. You get a score for sound and pace, and a dot under each word: green is good, orange needs another try.
  6. Mark your progress★ Mark verses to come back to, ✓ Learned when you know one. Under Settings → Student, add your name to keep progress and scores on any device.
  7. Read the meaningScroll down for the translation and Prabhupāda’s purport.
