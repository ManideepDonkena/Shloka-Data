
## Gamification ideas (not built)
Seen in other apps: Little Guru (gamified Sanskrit, subscription), Vedify (streaks, XP, levels, rewards, guided pronunciation for children), Practice Gita (quiz modes over all 700 verses), Sukulu/Streaklo-style streak freezes and rest days. "Prayog" could not be found by search; send a link if it is a specific app.
- **Streak** of days practised, with a weekly "rest day" or freeze so one miss does not feel like failure; no guilt copy.
- **Verse levels**: Words ✓ → Pādas ✓ → Halves ✓ → Verse ✓ → *Recited from memory* (recall mode scored by the mic). Each earns a star; four stars = learned.
- **Chapter map**: 18 chapters as a path/lotus; a chapter fills as verses are learned.
- **Points from effort, not perfection**: points per attempt, bonus for improving a verse's score or for revising an old one (spaced repetition).
- **Badges** with meaning: "First verse", "Chapter 2 complete", "7-day streak", "Karma-yoga" (all of 2.47-2.50)…
- **Quizzes between verses**: fill the missing word, order the words, match word to meaning (data already there), next-line recall.
- **Daily verse** (a small shared goal), and a **class leaderboard that is opt-in and shows effort (days, verses)** rather than rank by score; teacher assignments with a progress bar.
- **Gentle, respectful tone**: sacred text, so no confetti-over-the-verse or loss-aversion pressure.

## Sound-effect ideas (not built)
All short, soft, and off by default; build with Web Audio (no files) or a few tiny .ogg:
- **Your-turn cue** (exists: 660 Hz blip) and a lower "time's up" tone (exists).
- **Success** after a good attempt: two rising bell/kartāl-like notes; score ≥ 75. A softer single note for 50–74; silence (no buzzer) below.
- **Word-correct tick** as each green dot appears.
- **Verse learned**: a conch (śaṅkha) swell, 1 s. **Chapter done**: conch + tāmbūrā drone fade.
- **Streak kept**: a small mṛdaṅga tap; **new best**: a bell ring.
- **Tap on a word**: very quiet wooden tick. Respect the device's silent mode, add a master "Sound effects" switch and a volume.
- Optional ambient tānpūrā drone under practice (pitch-matched to the reference chant).
